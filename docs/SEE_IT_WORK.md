This is a short proof path through the existing public demonstrations. It is
not a second documentation set.

All examples are synthetic or controlled. They show what OSI-PIA can represent
or distinguish in a bounded setting; they do not establish claims about real
people, organizations, or populations.

## PIA: professional evidence

Start with the [Graph Evidence Tour](evidence/visualizations/GRAPH_EVIDENCE_TOUR.md), focusing on the basic PIA evidence chain and evidence convergence. The tour shows how source-grounded material can support a bounded capability interpretation without becoming a score or permanent label.

## OSI: organizational conditions

Read the synthetic cases to see how OSI distinguishes capability from the
conditions around it:

- [Capability Blockage](evidence/demonstrations/OSI_Evidence_Case_001_Capability_Blockage.md)
- [False Capability Signal](evidence/demonstrations/OSI_Evidence_Case_002_False_Capability_Signal.md)
- [Misattribution of Failure](evidence/demonstrations/OSI_Evidence_Case_003_Misattribution_of_Failure.md)

Continue to the [Live Sandbox Graph Tour](evidence/visualizations/LIVE_SANDBOX_GRAPH_TOUR.md) for controlled graph-projection evidence, then read [Project Status](PROJECT_STATUS.md) for what the demonstrations do and do not authorize.
