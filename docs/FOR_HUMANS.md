# For Humans

## How to think inside OSI

OSI is a governed knowledge architecture, not merely a software tool or a
collection of documents. Read the repository as a sequence of purposes:

- **Terminology** explains the words used consistently.
- **Principles** state the commitments that constrain design.
- **Governance** explains authority, stewardship, review, and promotion.
- **Research** records questions and experiments that remain exploratory.
- **Evidence** shows what has actually been demonstrated.
- **Foundation** holds mature conceptual artifacts.
- **History** records how ideas and decisions developed.

When documents disagree, check their lifecycle state and authority before
choosing a definition. Active research can challenge the foundation, but it
does not silently replace it. Evidence can support a claim, but it does not
become canonical without congruence, validation, and governance review.

Begin with the [OSI Philosophical Constitution](../foundation/OSI_CONSTITUTION.md)
and [Start Here](START_HERE.md), then use [Project Status](PROJECT_STATUS.md)
to understand what is implemented, demonstrated, or still gated.
