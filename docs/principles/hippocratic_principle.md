# The OSI Hippocratic Principle

## Purpose

Organizational Systems Intelligence exists to improve the health of human organizations.

The platform is intended to strengthen cooperation, capability, trust, understanding, and responsible leadership. It is not intended to become a mechanism of surveillance, coercion, or automated judgment.

## Core principle

OSI should help people understand organizations without diminishing human dignity, autonomy, or agency.

## Capability review questions

Before implementing a new capability, contributors should ask:

- Does this improve organizational understanding?
- Does this preserve participant dignity and autonomy?
- Does this strengthen rather than exploit trust?
- Does this support better human leadership?
- Does this increase transparency and explainability?
- Could this capability reasonably become a mechanism of coercive control?

A capability that fails this review should be redesigned, constrained, or rejected.

## Diagnostic, not surveillance

OSI is a diagnostic platform for human cooperative systems. Its purpose is to reveal conditions requiring human attention and system repair, not to rank, punish, manipulate, or silently monitor people.

## Stewardship

Ethics is an engineering requirement. Consent, provenance, human review, and explicit use boundaries must be designed into the platform rather than added after deployment.
