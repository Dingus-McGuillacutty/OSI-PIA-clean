# Research to Foundation Crosswalk

**Status:** Living document  
**Purpose:** Trace the evolution of research questions into canonical OSI-PIA
knowledge and preserve the audit trail of intellectual development.

## Purpose

The OSI-PIA repository intentionally distinguishes between research,
architecture, governance, ontology, and canonical knowledge.

This document records how research questions mature through evidence,
congruence, validation, governance, and promotion into the OSI foundation.

The Crosswalk is an audit bridge rather than an authority. It records what
occurred between active research and foundation artifacts, while history
records the surrounding project evolution. It does not independently authorize
promotion.

## Knowledge Transition Path

```text
active-research/
  ↓
Research Question
  ↓
Experiment / Prototype
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Congruence Review
  ↓
Validation
  ↓
Promotion
  ↓
Canonical Artifact
  ↓
Stewardship
  ↓
docs/history/
```

## Active Research Registry

### Foundational Research

- **RQ-001:** Can complex human cooperative systems be represented more
  accurately through multiple governed ontologies than through a single unified
  ontology?
- **RQ-002:** Can organizations be modeled more effectively through state
  transitions than through static organizational snapshots?
- **RQ-003:** Can organizational trust be represented as an ecological system
  property rather than solely an individual psychological attribute?
- **RQ-004:** Can Capability Capital be represented and measured more
  accurately than traditional Human Capital models?
- **RQ-005:** Can organizations be understood more accurately as ecological
  systems than as hierarchical structures?
- **RQ-006:** Can professional identity be represented through
  participant-governed evidence rather than résumé-centric or credential-centric
  models?
- **RQ-007:** Can congruence between multiple knowledge domains produce more
  explainable organizational intelligence than conceptual unification?
- **RQ-008:** Can knowledge lifecycle states improve governance,
  explainability, and AI reasoning compared to conventional document lifecycle
  management?
- **RQ-009:** Can evidence-governed AI produce more trustworthy assistance than
  prediction-first AI architectures?
- **RQ-010:** Can governed knowledge architectures exhibit developmental
  properties analogous to biological regulatory systems?
- **RQ-011:** Can the conditions that produce organizational behavior be
  represented more faithfully than the behaviors themselves?

## Promotion Criteria

A research question should normally demonstrate:

- sustained relevance;
- implementation experience;
- evidence;
- congruence;
- governance review;
- repeatability; and
- stewardship readiness.

Promotion represents governed confidence rather than permanent certainty.
