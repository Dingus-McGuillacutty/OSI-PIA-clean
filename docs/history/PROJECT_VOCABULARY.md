# Historical and Emergent Concepts

> These entries preserve how project concepts were understood during
> development. They are not authoritative definitions. For current formal
> meaning, use [Canonical Terminology](../../foundation/CANONICAL_TERMINOLOGY.md).

## Organizational Systems Intelligence (OSI)

Framework for observing, understanding, and improving the health of
human cooperative systems.

**Status:** Established

## Professional Identity Architecture (PIA)

Structured participant interview and narrative framework that supplies
ethically collected observational data to OSI.

**Status:** Established

## Capability Capital

The usable capability contained within people and relationships.
Capability is influenced by trust, motivation, and organizational
environment---not merely credentials.

**Status:** Working

## State Transition

A change in organizational condition over time. OSI focuses on state
transitions rather than static snapshots.

**Status:** Established

## Narrative Neutrality

Separate evidence from interpretation. Describe before explaining.

**Status:** Established

## Evidence → Observation → Interpretation

Core analytical workflow.

Evidence → raw facts

Observation → what is directly supported

Interpretation → proposed explanation

Confidence should accompany interpretation.

**Status:** Established

## Logic Chain

The complete, inspectable chain connecting evidence to recommendations
through explicit analytical transformations.

Logic Chains preserve Epistemic Integrity by ensuring that conclusions
remain explainable, reproducible, confidence-preserving,
uncertainty-preserving, and traceable to originating evidence.

A complete Logic Chain may connect:

Evidence → Observation → Interpretation → Inference → Conclusion → Recommendation

**Shorthand:**

> Follow the Logic Chain.

**Status:** Working

## Experiential Plurality

People can participate in the same organizational system while
experiencing, interpreting, and responding to it in materially different
ways.

PIA supports clearer individual self-understanding. OSI studies the
relationships, conditions, and state transitions of the larger
people-system without reducing organizational health to isolated
individual traits.

**Status:** Working

# Cultural Vocabulary

## Bubble Popper

A person who perceives observable behavior together with the structures,
mechanisms, constraints, incentives, relationships, and state
transitions that generate it.

The bubble popper sees the bubbles, the container, the bubble wand, and
the wand's shape and colors as parts of one coherent system.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Donkeyface

The temporary cognitive state experienced while crossing from conceptual
understanding into implementation architecture. It often feels like a
loss of competence, but usually indicates that several new abstractions
are being integrated at once.

**Practical meaning:** Pause, translate the layers in both directions,
and continue. Donkeyface frequently precedes a meaningful increase in
competence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Don't Build the Death Star

OSI should function as a diagnostic that helps organizations heal rather

## Jack--Daniel Principle

Inspired by *Stargate SG-1*.

### Jack Mode

Act decisively when evidence is sufficient and delay increases risk.

### Daniel Mode

Pause, investigate, and understand when uncertainty remains high.

OSI seeks wisdom in knowing when to transition between these modes.

Common shorthand:

-   "RUN, Jack!"
-   "Stop, Daniel."
