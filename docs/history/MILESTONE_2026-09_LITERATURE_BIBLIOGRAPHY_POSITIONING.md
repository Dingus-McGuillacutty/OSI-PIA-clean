# Milestone: Project Bibliography and PIA/OSI Literature Positioning

**Date:** 2026-09-16  
**Status:** Research-methodology milestone

## Summary

OSI-PIA established a governed literature intake and positioning layer. The
project bibliography consolidates sources cited by the public article series
and keeps a separate, explicitly unread set of research candidates for PIA and
OSI positioning. A comparison matrix and research note record where existing
literature is established, where OSI-PIA recombines or operationalizes ideas
differently, and which propositions remain open.

This makes source accumulation inspectable without turning a citation into an
endorsement, a familiar construct into a novelty claim, or a historical result
into a present-state fact.

## Development boundary

This milestone supports literature review, source provenance, temporal-scope
checking, and research-question formation. It does not claim that the listed
candidate sources have been independently reviewed, establish causal findings,
promote PIA or OSI constructs into canonical authority, or authorize product,
participant, organizational, hiring, or diagnostic use.

Candidate sources remain `unread` until their design, sample, findings,
limitations, supports, and does-not-support boundaries are recorded.
