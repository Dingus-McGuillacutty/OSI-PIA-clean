## v0.1 â€” 2026-07-17

### Conceptual milestone

OSI does not measure organizational health directly. Organizational health is treated as an emergent property inferred through recursive estimation of:

- topology;
- Predictability Field;
- Trust Field;
- organizational flow;
- state transitions.

This represented a shift from a metric-based model to a systems diagnostic model.
