# Developmental Milestone — Human-Centered Knowledge Ecology

## Summary

This milestone records a transition from viewing OSI-PIA as a collection of
repositories, documents, ontologies, and software toward viewing it as a
governed knowledge ecology. Knowledge development, research, implementation,
governance, publication, assurance, visualization, and computational reasoning
are treated as interacting developmental systems rather than independent
workflows.

## Major architectural advances

### Knowledge is developmental

Knowledge develops through governed state transitions represented by the
Knowledge Lifecycle and OSI Development Model.

### Multiple parallel lifecycles

Knowledge, research, evidence, software, publications, assurance, and
visualization may each have specialized states while sharing governance.

### Governed knowledge ecology

These lifecycles exchange evidence, terminology, provenance, decisions,
observations, and feedback. The architecture is therefore ecological rather
than a single linear workflow.

### Development grammar

Observation, Exploration, Formulation, Congruence, Validation, Promotion, and
Stewardship form a shared developmental grammar that specialized lifecycles can
inherit.

### Reflexive governance

Governance is architecturally intrinsic. Artifacts carry governance-related
information as part of their identity, but intrinsic governance never makes an
artifact self-authorizing. Human accountability, review, and stewardship remain
required.

### Human-centered computational architecture

Computational systems participate within the governed ecology. They do not
replace human judgment, responsibility, ethical reasoning, stewardship, or
meaning; their participation is bounded by architecture and governance.

### Development rather than automation

The primary objective is human development. Technology may accelerate learning,
reasoning, exploration, and stewardship, but should not replace human
cognitive development.

### Machine-readable governance

Repository organization communicates epistemic state:

- `foundation/` — canonical knowledge;
- `active-research/` — exploratory knowledge;
- `principles/` — governing constraints; and
- `governance/` — institutional authority.

### Research promotion path

Research progresses through Evidence → Congruence → Validation → Promotion →
Foundation → Stewardship. The Research-to-Foundation Crosswalk records this
developmental path.

## Emerging research

The following remain exploratory rather than canonical architecture:

- knowledge ecology;
- trust as cooperative capacity;
- predictability fields;
- multi-scale organizational trust;
- spherical knowledge representation;
- meta-nodes; and
- developmental knowledge systems.

## Architectural direction

The repository is evolving toward a governed knowledge operating ecology in
which humans remain the primary source of meaning and responsibility,
governance is intrinsic rather than appended, computational systems operate
within architectural boundaries, developmental processes remain transparent,
and interactions strengthen human capability whenever practical.

This milestone records a shift in architectural philosophy, not completion of a
software feature or promotion of these emerging research areas.
