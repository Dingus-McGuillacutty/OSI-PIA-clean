# Active Research Backlog

## Purpose

This backlog holds ideas that may deserve formal research questions but are not
yet sufficiently bounded, evidenced, or prioritized to receive an RQ
identifier. It is an intake queue for inquiry, not a list of commitments.

Items may be clarified, combined, deferred, rejected, or promoted into a
numbered research question through the normal research and governance process.

## Candidate topics

- Knowledge developmental biology analogy
- Multi-scale capability emergence
- Organizational metabolism metrics
- Human-AI cooperative governance
- Knowledge ecology

## Promotion boundary

An item should move from this backlog into `active-research/questions/` only
when it has a clear question, scope, assumptions, evidence or method proposal,
uncertainty, ethical implications, and criteria for validation or rejection.

Backlog inclusion does not imply endorsement, priority, truth, or future
promotion.
