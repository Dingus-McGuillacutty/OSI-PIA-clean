# RQ-010 — Governed Knowledge Architecture and Developmental Properties

## Research question

Can a governed knowledge architecture exhibit developmental properties
analogous to biological regulatory systems while remaining transparent,
explainable, and intentionally directed by human governance?

## Scope

This question concerns the behavior of the OSI/PIA knowledge architecture as
it moves through observation, exploration, formulation, congruence, validation,
promotion, and stewardship. The comparison to biological regulation is an
analogy for developmental behavior, not a claim that the repository or its
software is alive.

## What this may examine

- whether feedback, correction, and stewardship produce bounded developmental
  change;
- whether constraints and governance can remain visible while the system
  evolves;
- whether developmental patterns can be described without implying autonomy,
  intention, or authority beyond human governance; and
- whether the analogy improves explanation or introduces misleading
  anthropomorphism.

## Evidence and method

No conclusion is established by this question alone. Future work should use
versioned repository history, documented validation results, decision records,
and controlled synthetic experiments. Any proposed analogy must remain
traceable to observable system behavior and explicit human decisions.

## Boundaries and risks

This research does not authorize autonomous governance, self-directed system
goals, biological equivalence, participant inference, or production use. The
analogy must not obscure accountability, provenance, uncertainty, or the
ability of authorized humans to correct, supersede, or stop the process.

## Validation or rejection criteria

The question may advance only if the proposed developmental properties can be
described with reproducible evidence, clear limits, and a human-governed
feedback path. It should be rejected or narrowed if the analogy materially
reduces explainability, encourages anthropomorphic interpretation, or cannot be
distinguished from ordinary versioned governance and maintenance.
