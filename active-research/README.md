# OSI/PIA Research Workspace

## Purpose

This directory contains hypotheses, working methods, experiments, and
milestone observations that have not become architectural or ontological
commitments.

The documentation boundary and research standards are defined in
[`docs/research-standards/README.md`](../docs/research-standards/README.md).

## Current areas

- [`methodology/`](methodology/) contains working measurement and evidence
  methods.
- [`milestones/`](milestones/) records research-stage observations and project
  transitions.
- [`questions/`](questions/) contains proposed research questions and hypotheses
  that remain exploratory until evidence and validation justify advancement.
- [`RESEARCH_BACKLOG.md`](RESEARCH_BACKLOG.md) holds early ideas that are not
  yet mature enough to become numbered research questions.

## Ontology boundary

Research may propose, test, challenge, or refine concepts. Canonical concept
definitions belong in [`ontology/CORE CONCEPTS.md`](../ontology/CORE%20CONCEPTS.md),
and the rules for promoting and projecting ontology items belong in
[`ontology/META_ONTOLOGY.md`](../ontology/META_ONTOLOGY.md).

Moving a term into a graph catalogue or analytical script does not promote it.
Promotion requires the knowledge-lifecycle, congruence, governance, and
assurance work defined by the project architecture.

## Research record expectations

Research artifacts should identify:

- question or hypothesis;
- assumptions;
- evidence and provenance;
- method;
- uncertainty and limitations;
- ethical and participant implications;
- relationship to existing concepts;
- criteria for validation, rejection, or promotion.
