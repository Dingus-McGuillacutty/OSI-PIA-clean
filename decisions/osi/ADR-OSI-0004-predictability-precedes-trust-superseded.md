# ADR-OSI-0004: Predictability Precedes Trust

## Status

Superseded by
[ADR-OSI-0002](ADR-OSI-0002-predictability-as-precondition-of-trust.md).

## Normalization date

2026-07-23

## Decision

Model trust as an emergent property of the Predictability Field.

## Context

Earlier versions treated trust as a primary construct.

Discussion during Sprint 11 showed that trust is better understood as emerging from participants' ability to form reliable mental models of organizational behavior.

## Consequences

- Predictability becomes foundational.
- Trust becomes inferred.
- Measurement architecture becomes recursive.

## Supersession rationale

This concise record restates the same decision as ADR-OSI-0002 without its
scope, rationale, open questions, or date. It is retained as decision history;
ADR-OSI-0002 is the canonical authority.
