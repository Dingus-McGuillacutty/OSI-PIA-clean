# OSI Development Model

## Purpose

The OSI Development Model defines the governed process through which knowledge
is developed, tested, promoted, applied, and maintained within the OSI-PIA
ecosystem.

It operationalizes the epistemology of the platform.

The model does not define what OSI or PIA represent. Those responsibilities
belong to their respective meta-models.

Instead, it defines how observations, questions, propositions, models, methods,
and implementations may develop into knowledge that humans and machines can
use with justified confidence.

The model is platform-independent. It does not depend on GitHub, Markdown,
Neo4j, Python, artificial intelligence, or any particular implementation
technology.

---

## Foundational Claim

> Trustworthy knowledge is not produced by assertion alone. It develops through
> traceable, governed, and reversible transformations supported by evidence,
> congruence, validation, authority, and stewardship.

The Development Model treats knowledge development as an explicit function.

```text
Observation
    ↓
Exploration
    ↓
Formulation
    ↓
Congruence
    ↓
Validation
    ↓
Promotion
    ↓
Stewardship
    ↓
New Observation
